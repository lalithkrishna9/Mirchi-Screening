Booking Rules

Before you book

  1. Two tickets max. Each booking allows a maximum of 2 tickets — book for yourself and one guest.
  2. Confirmation by mail. Once your booking is submitted, you'll receive a confirmation email. Hold on to it — it's your entry pass.
  3. Waiting list. Purna Hall seats 130. Once we're full, further bookings move to the waiting list and will be notified if seats open up.
  4. Arrive on time. Seats are first-come, first-served within your confirmed slot between 12 PM and 4 PM on 16th September.
